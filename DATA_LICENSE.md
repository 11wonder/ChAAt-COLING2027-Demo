# Data and Benchmark Licensing

## ChAAt-authored diagnostic items

Of the 50-item de-duplicated diagnostic core, 47 items authored for ChAAt are
released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
(CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Attribution: Yankai Wan, Jilin University, 2026.

Three core items are explicit exceptions: `RX-06`, `RX-17`, and `RX-19` are
adapted from publicly accessible examination-preparation sources. They are
identified with their source URLs in the dataset, but are not relicensed under
CC BY 4.0 and remain subject to their original rights.

## Third-party source material

The 41-item exam-style tier is mostly adapted from public mock exams and
examination-preparation material. It is not represented as official Zhongkao or
Gaokao content. Those excerpts remain subject to their original rights and are
not relicensed by ChAAt. The public release may include source metadata, item
identifiers, and derived statistics without redistributing the underlying exam
excerpts.

Dictionary entries referenced from Zdic and Words.hk remain subject to their
respective terms. ChAAt stores short verification notes and links rather than
claiming ownership of those dictionary resources.
